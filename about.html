About

Always drawn to the arts, I took every class I could growing up around Atlanta, Georgia. Music was my first passion, but I also had a need to create on my own. After 2D and 3D design, ceramics, and block printing, I eventually found photography, where I learned to build pinhole cameras, develop film, and print my own photos in the darkroom. Photography became my second love after music, but somewhere along the way I lost both.

In early 2022, I moved into a dreamy 120-year-old rental on Södermalm, where I lived alone for the better part of three years. There, I spent most of my free time returning to things I'd loved and lost over the years, trying a few new ones, and found myself behind a camera again.

"I am out with lanterns, looking for myself."

Emily Dickinson

Unedited 35mm photos shot with Olympus OM-1, Olympus Trip 35, and Nikon L35AF.
